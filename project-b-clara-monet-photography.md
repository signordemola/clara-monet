## Industry Context — What You Need to Know

### The UK Portrait Photography Market

The UK photography market is valued at approximately £1.9 billion. It is dominated by
small owner-led studios — photographers who shoot, edit, admin and market entirely on their
own. The business model is project-based: each client is a multi-week engagement, not a
slot on a calendar. This is the core difference from Project A.

### The Real Tool Stack Solo UK Photographers Run

| Tool | Purpose | Monthly Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Squarespace / WordPress | Public website | £13–£23 |
| Acuity / Calendly | Scheduling | £12–£20 |
| HoneyBook / Dubsado / Studio Ninja | Contracts, questionnaires, invoices | £29–£45 |
| Pic-Time / Pixieset / ShootProof | Gallery delivery | £10–£20 |
| Wave / FreshBooks | Accounting | £0–£12 |
| Stripe / PayPal / Bank transfer | Payments | 1.5–3% per transaction |
| **Total** | | **£64–£120+ / month** |

None of these tools share data with each other. Every stage requires manual action.
When a client asks "where are my photos?" the photographer checks three apps to answer.

### The Gap No Single Tool Closes

Every CRM (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Studio Ninja) handles contracts and invoices.
Every gallery tool (Pic-Time, Pixieset) handles delivery and download.
No single tool connects inquiry → booking → contract → deposit → questionnaire →
session → gallery → balance invoice → review request as one unbroken thread.
That thread is what this demo builds.

### UK Legal Standards You Must Know

**Copyright:** Under UK law, the photographer automatically owns the copyright to every
image taken. The client receives a **licence** — not ownership. The contract must state
what the licence covers: personal use, commercial use, social media, print. This is
non-negotiable and must appear in every UK photography contract.

**Model release:** The photographer cannot use images in their portfolio or marketing
without explicit consent from the client. This is captured as a checkbox in the contract
(not a separate form). Clients can opt out and the system must record their preference.

**UK GDPR / ICO:** UK photographers must register with the ICO (Information
Commissioner's Office) and comply with UK GDPR. This means: only collect data
necessary for the service, get explicit consent for data storage, allow clients to request
deletion, store data securely. The contract includes a data handling clause. The intake
form includes a GDPR consent checkbox. The system stores consent timestamps.

**MTD (Making Tax Digital):** Mandatory from April 2026 for UK sole traders.
Not relevant to the demo build, but worth knowing when talking to UK photographer clients.

### How the Booking Process Actually Works (Industry Standard)

**Inquiry first — not direct booking.** At this price point (£175–£400+ per session),
UK portrait photographers review every inquiry before sending a booking link. Direct
booking (pick a slot, pay immediately) is used for mini sessions and headshot studios
only. For personal brand, family portrait and bespoke work, the photographer assesses
fit before committing.

**Response time matters.** Photographers who respond within 5 minutes of an inquiry
convert at 9× the rate of those who respond in an hour. An automated acknowledgement
email sent immediately on inquiry submission is a conversion tool, not a courtesy.

**Deposit language:** The deposit is called a **booking fee** or **retainer** — not
simply "deposit." This matters because it signals the fee is held against the
photographer's reserved time, not just the session itself. Industry standard: 25–50%
non-refundable booking fee. This demo uses 25%.

**Turnaround standard:** Headshots and portraits: 1–2 weeks. Personal branding
sessions: 2–3 weeks. This must appear in the contract and in the post-session email.
Clients' most common complaint is not knowing when to expect their gallery.

**Balance payment timing:** Industry best practice: invoice sent simultaneously with
the gallery link. Client receives both in the same email. Gallery is immediately
accessible. Invoice due within 7 days. This is the trust-building approach. Withholding
the gallery until payment creates friction and damages the client relationship at the
moment they are most excited — right after delivery.

**Review requests:** Reviews are the number one lead generation channel for
photographers. A review request email is sent 4 days after gallery delivery — enough
time for the client to have looked through their images and felt something about them.

**Gallery tools:** Pic-Time is preferred by established UK portrait photographers for its
premium presentation and print store integrations. Pixieset is popular for its clean,
simple client experience. ShootProof and WeTransfer are also used. The system is
gallery-tool agnostic — the owner pastes any link, the system delivers it.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Contract Clauses (UK Standard — All Must Appear in Demo Contract)

1. Parties: photographer full name + client full name, date
2. Scope of work: session type, date, time, location, number of edited images, delivery format
3. Turnaround time: stated clearly per session type
4. Delivery method: online gallery link via email (platform: Pic-Time / Pixieset)
5. Payment terms: total fee, booking fee amount + non-refundable status, balance amount + due date
6. Late payment: 8% annual interest on overdue balances (UK Late Payment of Commercial Debts Act)
7. Cancellation: client policy (below)
8. Copyright: photographer retains full copyright under UK Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988
9. Licence granted to client: personal and commercial use, social media, website, print — non-exclusive, non-transferable
10. RAW files: not included in any package
11. Model release: client consent for photographer to use images in portfolio and marketing (opt-in checkbox)
12. GDPR / data handling: data collected for the purpose of this booking only, stored securely, client right to erasure
13. Liability: photographer not liable for events outside reasonable control (equipment failure, illness)
14. Governing law: England and Wales

### Cancellation Policy (UK Standard)

| Notice given | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 14+ days before session | Full booking fee refunded |
| 7–14 days before | 50% of booking fee refunded |
| Under 7 days | Booking fee forfeited in full |
| Client no-show | Booking fee forfeited, session may be rescheduled at Clara's discretion |
| Clara cancels | Full booking fee refunded + alternative date offered |

*Note: UK portrait photography uses a more generous cancellation window than beauty/wellness
(14 days vs 48 hours) because sessions are planned weeks in advance and rescheduling
a portrait session has lower financial risk than a beauty appointment.*

### Pre-Session Questionnaire (Industry Standard — Sent 48hrs After Contract Signed)

1. What is the main purpose of this session? (LinkedIn, website, personal brand, social media, family memories, other)
2. Who will be attending the session? (names, ages if children)
3. Do you have a preferred location? (Clara's recommendation / outdoor London / specific area / your choice)
4. What mood or feeling do you want from the images? (professional and polished / warm and natural / bold and creative / relaxed and candid)
5. What are your outfit plans? How many changes? Any colours to avoid?
6. Do you have a Pinterest board or inspiration images to share? (URL or upload)
7. Where will you primarily use these images? (website, LinkedIn, Instagram, printed materials, all of the above)
8. Is there anything I should know before the day? (camera anxiety, mobility, timing constraints, children's nap times)
9. Any specific shots or moments that are important to you?
10. Are you happy for your images to be featured in my portfolio and social media? (yes / yes with approval / no — model release)

## Tech Stack

### Core Framework
- **TanStack Start v1** — full-stack React, server functions, file-based routing, Vite
- **TanStack Router** — type-safe routing, typed params, typed search params
- **React 19**
- **TypeScript** — strict mode

> TanStack Start is chosen here over Next.js because the application is a client portal
> and owner dashboard — no public SEO landing page is being built (Clara's Squarespace
> site stays). TanStack Start's server functions replace the need for separate API routes,
> keeping the codebase leaner. The API is stable at RC/v1 and production-ready for
> pinned dependencies.

### Data & Validation
- **Prisma** — ORM, schema-first, migrations
- **PostgreSQL** — Railway or Neon (local: Docker)
- **Zod** — all input validation, form schemas, server function payloads

### Client State & Fetching
- **TanStack Query v5** — server state, background refetch, cache invalidation
- **Zustand** — multi-step wizard state for booking and questionnaire flows

### UI
- **shadcn/ui** — base component library
- **Tailwind CSS v4**
- **Radix UI** — via shadcn
- **Lucide React** — icons
- **date-fns** — all date/time calculations and formatting

### Payments
- **Stripe** — booking fee + balance payment, refunds, webhook verification
- **@stripe/stripe-js + @stripe/react-stripe-js** — Stripe Elements

### Email
- **Resend** — transactional email delivery
- **React Email + @react-email/components** — typed, component-based email templates

### Auth (Owner only)
- **iron-session** — encrypted cookie session, no OAuth overhead
- Single owner: password login

### Utilities
- **nanoid** — booking token + session token generation
- **pdf-lib** — generate signed contract PDF for email attachment after signing
- **date-fns-tz** — timezone-aware scheduling for reminder emails (Europe/London)

```prisma
  reminder48hSentAt     DateTime?

  outfitPlans     String?  // outfit description and number of changes
  moodBoardUrl    String?  // Pinterest or inspiration link
  avoidsNotes     String?  // poses, angles or anything to avoid
  imageUsage      String?  // where images will be used
  additionalNotes String?  // anything else
  modelRelease    Boolean  @default(false)  // confirmed at questionnaire (mirrors contract)
  submittedAt     DateTime @default(now())
}

// ─── Cron Log ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

model CronLog {
  id        String   @id @default(cuid())
  type      String   // "questionnaire_prompt" | "questionnaire_reminder" |
                     // "reminder_48h" | "reminder_24h" | "balance_reminder" |
                     // "review_request"
  jobId     String
  sentAt    DateTime @default(now())
  success   Boolean
  error     String?
}
```

## Key Implementation Notes

**Why inquiry-first matters for the demo**
Most portfolio demos show direct booking (pick a slot, pay, done). The inquiry-first
flow is more realistic for UK portrait photographers at this price point and it shows
the Growth tier's true complexity — the owner is part of the flow, not just the
infrastructure. This is what differentiates ShootFlow from "a booking page."

**The job lifecycle is the core concept**
Everything in this system — emails, stage badges, owner actions, client portal content —
derives from `job.stage`. The stage machine (`lib/stages.ts`) is the engine of the
entire application. It must be written and tested before any UI is built on top of it.

**Token architecture — two tokens, two purposes**
- `bookingToken`: lives on the `Inquiry`. Generated at approval. Used only during the
  booking flow (contract + deposit). Short-lived in intent, but not expired server-side
  in demo scope — mark inquiry as BOOKED to invalidate.
- `sessionToken`: lives on the `Job`. Generated at booking confirmation (in Stripe webhook).
  Permanent. Used for the client portal for the life of the job.
Never expose database IDs in client-facing URLs.

**Gallery delivery is intentionally simple**
The system does not integrate with Pic-Time or Pixieset via API. Clara pastes any gallery
URL. The system records the URL, sends the email, advances the stage. This is the correct
design because gallery tools change — clients switch between Pic-Time, Pixieset, and
WeTransfer regularly. The system is agnostic. When demoing, use a real Pixieset or
Pic-Time test gallery URL for authenticity.

**Balance payment never withholds the gallery**
The gallery URL is sent in the same email as the invoice. The client can view and
download their images immediately. The invoice follows. This is the UK industry standard
at this price point. Withholding the gallery is a lower-trust model and creates friction
at the moment the client relationship is at its best.

**Contract PDF generation**
pdf-lib generates the signed contract as a PDF and attaches it to the booking-confirmed
email. The client receives a permanent copy. Clara has a permanent record. The system
does not need to store the PDF — it is generated once at signing and emailed. If needed
for demo purposes, store in `/tmp` or generate on demand from job data.

**GDPR notes for the demo**
The demo stores `gdprConsentAt` and `marketingConsent` as separate fields on `Client`.
In a production build, this would connect to a proper data retention policy and ICO
registration. For the demo, the consent timestamp is enough to demonstrate awareness.
Mention this in the case study — it signals you know the legal landscape, not just
the technology.
